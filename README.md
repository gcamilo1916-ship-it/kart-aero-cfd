# Racing Kart Aero-Package Development 🏎️💨

**Role:** Lead Design Engineer<!--[cite: 3] -->  
**Timeline:** 2025 - Present<!--[cite: 3] -->

**Overview**  
Spearheading the aerodynamic optimization of a racing kart chassis front wing utilizing S1223 multi-element airfoils and NACA profiles.<!--[cite: 3] --> Originally conceived as an advanced independent study to understand the core mechanics of Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and master SolidWorks modeling, the project serves as a hands-on exploration of fluid behaviors, aerodynamic efficiency, and cornering grip optimization.

**Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)**  
Simulating airflow, downforce, and drag at specific ride heights using CFD.<!--[cite: 3] --> The goal is to extract highly accurate data on boundary layers and pressure distribution across the S1223 front wing profile, iterating designs to minimize drag and control vorticity.

**Finite Element Analysis (FEA)**  
Executing FEA workflows to validate structural integrity prior to fabrication.<!--[cite: 3] --> This guarantees that the high aerodynamic loads generated do not induce material failure or excessive wing deflection at the mounting points.
